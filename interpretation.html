Digital Interpretation

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Digital Interpretation is a series of colour digital works about how everything we see online is, in one way or another, an interpretation of reality. Unlike the other rooms, these are digital works, not silver gelatin prints. They hang in a panelled Paris salon.

Size and edition on request; please inquire.
