Carpathian

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Carpathian is a series of black and white film photographs of the Carpathian Mountains. The prints hang in an Alpine hall with a larch ceiling and a long window onto a valley, each a fiber-based silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
