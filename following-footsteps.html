Following Footsteps

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Following the Footsteps of Ancestors is a series of black and white photographs made on film. The prints hang in a panelled Paris salon, each a fiber-based silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
