Layers of History

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Layers of History is a series of photographs made in Grand Canyon National Park in 2024 that treat geology as a kind of timekeeping. Without colour, the eye moves through form, strata and shadow. The prints hang in a panelled Paris salon, each a silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
