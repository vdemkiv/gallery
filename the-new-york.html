The New York

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

The New York gathers black and white fragments from a city that is always in motion: pauses inside the grid, late-night streets, winter light and solitary figures. The prints hang in a New York storefront gallery with shop windows onto a brick street, each a silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
