Lost

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Lost takes place high in the mountains and follows people who seek solace and a new path in remote places. The prints hang in an Alpine hall with a long window onto a valley, each a silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
