Pacific

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Pacific is a meditation on water and its connection to the Pacific Ocean. In black and white, the surface of the water becomes almost abstract. The prints hang in a concrete pavilion in the Los Angeles hills, each a silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
