Bizkaia

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Bizkaia was made in the mountains and valleys of the Basque Country. The pictures think about distance, belonging and the steadying force of landscape. They hang in a sandstone hall in Bilbao with tall windows onto water and green hills, each a silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
