860519

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

860519 looks at the impact of urban infrastructure on the natural environment, and in particular at the vast network of roads and the presence of cars in the United States. The prints hang in a Miami beach gallery with a glass wall onto the sea, each a silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
