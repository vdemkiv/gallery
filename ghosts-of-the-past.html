Ghosts of the Past

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Ghosts of the Past photographs the ruined Church of All Saints in Godovytsia, in the Lviv region of Ukraine, a late baroque church designed by Bernard Meretyn whose altar once held Johann Georg Pinsel's Sacrifice of Abraham. The prints hang in a London warehouse gallery, each a silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
