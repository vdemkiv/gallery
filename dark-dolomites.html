Dark Dolomites

Volodymyr Demkiv · 3D gallery room

Dark Dolomites captures the raw, dramatic atmosphere of these ancient mountains in high-contrast black and white, and the relationship between nature and people. The prints hang in an Alpine hall with a long window onto the mountains, each a silver gelatin print made by hand by the artist.

Each print is made by hand by the artist in the darkroom, on fiber-based paper, signed and numbered in an edition of 5 + 1 AP. Prints are available; please inquire.
